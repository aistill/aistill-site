What intracellular micronutrient testing adds

SpectraCell Micronutrient Testing is designed to look at functional intracellular nutrient status. Instead of only counting molecules in serum at one point in time, the panel evaluates how cells perform in culture when specific nutrients are limited or restored.

This does not make it a replacement for standard bloodwork. It can be complementary and more granular, especially when a clinician wants to understand functional patterns across vitamins, minerals, amino acids, antioxidants, fatty acids, and related metabolites.

Clinical distinction

  • Serum testing: measures concentration in blood at the time of draw.
  • Functional intracellular testing: evaluates cellular performance patterns related to nutrient availability.
  • Best use: reviewed alongside symptoms, medications, history, and conventional labs by a licensed clinician.

The OsteopathicAI layer

The Hub is not designed to hand patients a diagnosis or a product plan. It is designed to organize the panel, the person's context, and the next clinical conversation.

That means the report emphasizes pattern recognition, relationship-centered talking points, uncertainty, and appropriate clinician accountability.

Whole-person inputs

  • Biology: functional micronutrient patterns and related categories.
  • Context: symptoms, lifestyle, medications, goals, and current care setting.
  • Conversation: questions that help the patient and clinician decide what matters next.
